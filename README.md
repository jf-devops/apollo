# Apollo - AI Platform

## What is Apollo?

Apollo is a commercial fork of [Open WebUI](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui), maintained by **Just-Forward Consulting GmbH** for enterprise and client deployments. Built on the foundation of Open WebUI's powerful AI interface technology, Apollo provides a robust, self-hosted AI platform designed for commercial use cases.

## About Just-Forward Consulting GmbH

Just-Forward Consulting GmbH specializes in AI solutions and enterprise software deployment. We leverage open-source technologies like Open WebUI to create customized AI platforms for our clients, providing both commercial and non-commercial AI solutions tailored to specific business needs.

Our expertise includes:
- Enterprise AI platform deployment
- Custom AI solution development
- Self-hosted AI infrastructure
- AI integration consulting
